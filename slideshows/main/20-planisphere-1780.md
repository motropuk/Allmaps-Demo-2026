A *Planisphere suivant la projection de Mercator* from about 1780, held at Sterling, georeferenced with sixty control points and draped around the planet.

The point: this is not only a tool for nineteenth-century city surveys. It works for early-modern material, for manuscript maps, for the kinds of things this library collects at depth.
