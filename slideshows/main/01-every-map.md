What you are looking at is the **City of New Haven** as surveyed by A. B. Hill in about 1893, one of the Beinecke's maps. It is being drawn right now, in this browser, straight from Yale Digital Collections' own IIIF image server and bent to fit a modern basemap underneath. Nothing was exported, nothing was pre-rendered, and no copy of the image was made.

That is the whole idea in one picture. Yale has spent years digitizing maps and publishing them as IIIF images. [Allmaps](https://allmaps.org) takes those images, exactly as we already serve them, and gives them back to us as maps: things you can overlay, compare, measure and build on.

Drag the map. Scroll to zoom. Hold **Space** to see the modern streets underneath. Use the arrow keys to move through the story.
