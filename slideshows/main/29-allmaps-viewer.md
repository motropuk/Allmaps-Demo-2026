The **Viewer** is where anyone can look at a georeferenced map on a modern basemap. This is the 1911 Saloon map, live. The buttons at the bottom are the controls you have seen in these slides: **opacity**, and the **magic pen** that removes the paper. The strip at the top opens the source information, with the link back to Yale Digital Collections.
