Switch to a **thin-plate spline** and the chart is warped smoothly through every control point. The white lines vanish and the coast settles onto the modern one.

The transformation type is stored in the annotation and chosen map by map. Go back a slide to compare: a closer fit at the points also changes the shapes between them.
