Allmaps began in 2021 as a collaboration between TU Delft Library and Bert Spaan. It ran on grants until the NEH award that supported it was terminated in April 2025; the response was to put it on a sustainable footing inside the IIIF Consortium.

The IIIF Executive Committee approved the **Allmaps–IIIF Partnership** in October 2025. It runs for three years, 2026 to 2028, and Consortium members join at two levels: Supporter ($2,000) and Innovator ($10,000). Yale is an Innovator, with a seat on the Advisory Board, early access to new features and a say in the roadmap; Jon Manton chairs that board. The Beinecke and TU Delft Library funded the initial Supporter benefits.

<aside class="callout">

<h2>What you can do on Monday</h2>

**Georeference a map.** Paste a Digital Collections manifest URL into [editor.allmaps.org](https://editor.allmaps.org). Ten minutes for a city plan.

**Use one.** Open any annotation in [viewer.allmaps.org](https://viewer.allmaps.org), or drop its tile URL into QGIS.

**Tell DCA.** Thirteen thousand candidates, sixty-two done. New Haven, Connecticut and the Beinecke's early-modern charts are the obvious next sets.

</aside>
