Because our maps are delivered through IIIF, every one of them is already in Allmaps. Open [editor.allmaps.org/organizations/yale](https://editor.allmaps.org/organizations/yale), pick any map, and start placing points; nothing to install, no account to create. Every georeferenced map shows a *Yale University Library* badge with a link back to Digital Collections wherever it appears.

Four apps, all in the browser:

- [**Editor**](https://editor.allmaps.org/organizations/yale): the four steps you have just seen.
- [**Viewer**](https://viewer.allmaps.org/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fannotations.allmaps.org%2Fmaps%2F04d980dd9546824d): any georeferenced map on a modern basemap, with opacity, the magic pen and colorize as buttons.
- [**Here**](https://here.allmaps.org/): open it on your phone and it shows the historical maps of wherever you are standing. Worth trying on campus: at the Beinecke it finds our New Haven maps beside Boston Public Library's and David Rumsey's.
- [**Latest**](https://latest.allmaps.org/): the live stream of maps being georeferenced right now, by the Bodleian, the Bibliothèque nationale, Texas A&M, the National Library of Scotland, and us.

We have been working with Julia Hernández at the Beinecke on using Allmaps in classroom teaching with these collections.
