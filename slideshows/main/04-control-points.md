The pink dots are **ground control points**. Each one pairs a pixel on the scan with a longitude and latitude on the earth: a street corner here, the same street corner on a modern map. Five points were enough for this plan; the 1893 Hill map you saw first has forty.

Zoom in to see where Doolittle's corners were matched. Placing points is the third step in the Editor, and the only one that takes real attention.
